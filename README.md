## 01 - Perspective

I work at the intersection of AI research and engineering, with interests in privacy-aware machine learning, language-model applications, and systems that help people solve practical problems.

My approach is grounded in **careful experimentation, sound implementation, and clear technical trade-offs**. I value work that goes beyond a convincing demo: systems should be understandable, useful, and built with their real constraints in mind.

<details>
<summary><strong>Principles behind my work</strong></summary>

- **Start with the problem.** Understand the user, context, data, and constraints before choosing a model or framework.
- **Test assumptions.** Prefer evaluation and evidence over novelty for its own sake.
- **Build for use.** Turn technical ideas into implementations people can interact with and assess.
- **Respect the data.** Consider privacy, reliability, and limitations as part of the design.

</details>
